    Vorabpauschale estimates

    Taxes → Vorabpauschale estimates the annual fund amount and its German private-investor tax charge. Select a calculation year, broker/accounts and ETFs. The result includes capital gains tax, solidarity surcharge and optional church tax. Results come first; Filter selects accounts and funds, Tax settings contains the allowance and church tax, and Fund details → Edit inputs opens overrides for one fund. It does not book transactions or claim to reproduce a bank's tax statement.

    Completed years use ownership at 31 December. The current-year forecast keeps current shares and the latest available price unchanged through year-end, with no future trades. It uses observed distributions plus any additional expected amount entered in Edit inputs. The price date and forecast basis remain visible. The supported calculation years are 2018–2026; a new year needs its own published rate, never the previous year's rate.

    Inputs and completeness

    Applied history supplies acquisition months, remaining quantities, FIFO sales and transfers. Acquisition months follow recorded transaction dates, not settlement dates; verify imported dates if the source uses booking dates. Purchase prices and fees are not needed. Shares bought during the year receive the remaining calendar months' fraction, including their purchase month. Sold shares do not contribute. Transfers between your own depots retain original acquisition dates. FIFO is calculated within a depot/share class before combining accounts; allocation sleeves are not separate tax depots.

    Year-start and year-end redemption prices are the legally preferred fund prices. Market prices are the statutory fallback when no redemption price is set (or it is not set continuously at least monthly). Otherwise the app's market prices are an explicitly labelled approximation. Edit inputs accepts EUR per-share price overrides and an evidence source or assumption. Foreign-currency market observations are converted using dated ECB reference rates, with the last published rate on or before non-publication days (at most seven days old).

    Distributions mean the fund's whole-year gross distributions per share, including payments before you acquired shares. They are not just your personal net cash receipts. Automatic uses downloaded gross payouts for distributing funds and zero payouts for a verified accumulating share class. The calculator reuses the app's existing issuer metadata and private verified income-policy overrides by exact share class. The verification date records when metadata was checked, not when the share-class policy began. Calculations use that known policy across the selected history; supply a year-specific override if the policy changed. Its source and verification date are shown in Fund details. Conflicting payout observations require review.

    Downloaded fund observations remain separate from transactions. An empty download without a known accumulating policy does not prove zero. Resolve missing inputs opens the affected fund. Select Accumulating / no distributions when applicable, enter a Manual total, or explicitly select Market history to use the observed amount as an assumption. Manual overrides take precedence. For a forecast, a manual total is observed distributions so far; additional expected distributions are added once. Market distributions use provider event dates, which may differ from payment dates. Supply the annual EUR total for a more precise calculation. A quote in subunits such as pence does not establish dividend units; ambiguous downloaded distributions remain unavailable until reviewed.

    Prices, distributions and quantities must use consistent share units. Downloads reverse provider split normalization and normalize to the calculation boundary. Conflicting split evidence requires review. Missing acquisition dates, unexplained balance replacements or unsupported events leave the affected result unavailable. An applied confirmation that ownership began with the first recorded purchase establishes zero holdings before that purchase. An ordinary later balance snapshot does not establish earlier ownership, even when its recorded balance is zero. Reviewed remaining lots can supply a calculator-only assumption without changing history; changing the history requires another review. Historical selection includes zero-share and retained closed positions. Unlinked histories need their fund status reviewed and may lack a usable name or price listing.

    Filter → Include unclassified funds exposes positions whose investment-fund status is not known. Confirm Investment fund in their review before including their amounts. An ETF name or an investment taxonomy does not establish tax classification.

    Optional tax settings

    Teilfreistellung defaults to None / not applied. Select the applicable rate per fund/year; it is shared across that share class's depots. The ordinary private investor rates are 30% for equity funds, 15% for mixed funds, 60% for property funds and 80% for foreign property funds. These are legal fund classifications, not allocation categories or the industries represented by an ETF.

    Church tax defaults to zero; 8% and 9% are available. Its deduction reduces the capital gains tax base rate through the statutory formula. Solidarity surcharge is included. Loss offsets, foreign-tax credits and personal income-tax assessment are not modelled.

    Available allowance is the amount still available for the selected scope in the receipt year, which follows the calculation year. It defaults to €0 and is applied once to the combined taxable amount. Changing the selection or year resets it; fund rows show tax before allowance. Do not reuse the same available allowance in independent estimates and then add the after-allowance tax totals.

    The headline is a partial subtotal whenever selected funds remain unavailable. The table identifies the gaps; missing results are never silently treated as zero. All results remain estimates, including calculations with manually supplied fund prices. Special cases such as mergers, liquidations, transfers to another owner, changes of residence or changes of tax classification need separate review.

    Calculation and official sources

    For per-share year-start price P0, year-end price P1, gross annual distributions D and the year's Basiszins b:

    base = min(P0 × 0.70 × b, P1 − P0 + D)
annual amount = max(0, base − D)
lot amount = annual amount × remaining shares × acquisition-month factor

    The factor is (13 − purchase month) / 12 for purchases during the year, otherwise one. Sum unrounded contributions and round once per depot/share class to cents. Apply the selected Teilfreistellung, then deduct the available allowance once. With church-tax rate k, capital gains tax is tax base / (4 + k), church tax is that tax times k, and solidarity surcharge is 5.5% of that tax. The surcharge truncates fractions of a cent. Displayed monetary estimates use cent precision; bank posting and aggregation conventions can differ.

    The rules and annual parameters use only official German documentation:

    • §18 InvStG: calculation, monthly reduction and receipt.
    • BMF, 21 May 2019, paragraphs 18.2–18.12: precision, currency conversion and prices.
    • §20 InvStG: Teilfreistellung.
    • §32d EStG: tax and church-tax formula, §4 SolzG: solidarity surcharge.
    • Bundesbank: relevant annual Basiszins series, using the first published daily observation of each calculation year.
    • BMF 2025 rate: 2.53%, receipt 2 January 2026.
    • BMF 2026 rate: 3.20%, receipt 4 January 2027.

    Saved overrides, optional settings and reviewed lots live in the private tax-inputs.json; fetched observations live under .cache/tax/. Saves use the workspace's normal revision checks and backups. Market refreshes never overwrite manual inputs. The feature loads and calculates only when Taxes is open.
