    Positions and buy-ins

    Add or edit a holding

    In Positions, choose Add position. Search for an investment and use Select investment, or Choose listing when several exchanges are grouped under a verified ISIN. Verify the exchange and trading currency. Enter manually opens identity fields without requiring search. Change investment reopens search while retaining your quantity, cost and allocation draft. Select an existing instrument when recording it in another account so its identity is reused. The name and price ticker appear together in one row. You can edit the name; a searched ticker is read-only. Use Change investment for another listing. Saved holdings use Connect live prices to review listing changes. Explicit manual entry permits typing a ticker. Optional ISIN metadata is retained under More details; provider omissions do not prevent ticker-based pricing. Names alone do not identify the same security.

    Select a row for details; use its pencil to edit. A rename applies to all account rows for that instrument. Quantities can be fractional and nonnegative. Keep account/portfolio distinctions when the same instrument is held more than once. Enter quantity explicitly; zero keeps a planned position. Decimal points or commas are accepted without thousands separators. Average and total buy-in are visible together, followed by category and its target side by side. Account/broker, manual pricing and other optional fields are under More details. Physical assets using manual valuation keep their current price visible.

    Buy-in and targets are optional. A successful save writes to the active private workspace. A stale form is rejected; reload it before saving again.

    For assets without provider prices, supply a manual unit price with its currency, date and quantity unit. A manual price overrides provider quotes; clearing it restores provider pricing. Instrument type and underlying exposure are distinct: an ETF may hold equity, non-equity or an unknown mixture.

    Record the cost of the remaining holding

    Enter Average buy-in per unit or Total buy-in, with the acquisition currency. The other cost updates automatically. The last cost field you edited stays fixed when quantity changes; the caption identifies it. Clear buy-in removes both amounts. Unchanged saved amounts retain their precision.

    Total buy-in is the cost of the quantity still held, including purchase fees; it is not lifetime deposits. The app divides total cost by quantity to obtain average cost. Positive total cost needs a positive quantity. Blank cost remains unknown and does not prevent allocation analysis.

    New buy-ins default to the portfolio reporting currency; existing records keep their original currency. Quote currency and buy-in currency can differ. For a foreign-currency buy-in, supply a purchase date, exchange rate or converted total cost. Rates are labelled as reporting-currency units per original currency unit. Missing information can be saved, with gains excluded and a warning. Latest-FX approximations require explicit confirmation and remain labelled.

    Use a single date only when it applies to the whole cost. For purchases on different dates, enter separate purchase rows or supply their combined converted cost. After sales, transfers or splits, reconcile the remaining quantity and cost with your records. This summary editor does not infer sales or realized gains. Use transaction history to record sales and calculate FIFO carrying costs and realized gains; it is not a tax-lot reporting service.

    Replace balances

    Use Positions → Update balances for several current summaries at once. Review quantities and optional buy-ins, select average or total cost entry, then confirm and save the snapshot. Repeating a balance snapshot replaces quantities; it does not add purchases. Targets and classifications stay saved. Quantity confirmation dates are distinct from purchase dates and market-price timestamps. Changed quantities or buy-ins replace active cost components and invalidate incompatible conversions; they do not infer sales or splits. Metadata-only edits preserve conversions.

    Calculate a new holding from purchases

    In Add position → More details, enable Calculate from purchases. Enter one row per purchase with quantity, unit price, optional date and optional fees. An optional FX rate records reporting-currency units per buy-in currency unit; otherwise dated purchases use historical conversion when available. Missing conversions exclude gains, while available current values remain included. Use one buy-in currency throughout. The quantity and cost fields above become read-only and show the calculated result, including fees. Missing purchase prices keep the resulting cost unknown. Invalid rows must be corrected before saving.

    Disabling purchase entry restores the manual quantity and cost draft; purchase rows remain available for this session. Closing and resuming the dialog retains the draft. Cancel discards it. Save position saves the holding, metadata and purchase records together once. These must be purchases making up the current holding without intervening sales or splits; sales accounting is not supported. For additional purchases into an existing position, use Bulk add purchases. An activated transaction history routes purchases to its history editor instead.

    Add a purchase batch

    Open Bulk add purchases for one instrument/account/portfolio. Enter rows, paste spreadsheet cells or upload a UTF-8 CSV/TSV. These figures are invented:

    date,shares,price,fees
2026-01-01,2,100,1
2026-02-01,3,120,2

    This batch adds five shares and costs 563 currency units, averaging 112.60 per share. Dates are optional ISO dates; quantities must be positive. Blank fees are zero; blank prices are unknown. Use one selected currency per batch; later batches may use different currencies. The optional fx_rate column supplies a conversion rate to the current reporting currency for each row. Mixed-currency totals are displayed in reporting currency; original amounts remain saved. Decimal points or commas are supported without thousands separators; quote decimal commas in comma-delimited CSV or use tabs/semicolons.

    Add new purchases increases shares. Calculate buy-in for shares already held leaves quantity unchanged and requires purchases accounting for all current shares with no intervening sales or splits. Review the before/after preview, then save once. Repeated batches require acknowledgment; identical rows within one batch all count. Unknown opening cost or a missing purchase price keeps the resulting cost unknown.

    After a balance replacement, additional purchase dates must be after the balance confirmation date. Retained batches are context, not a complete transaction ledger; do not sum old and recalculated batches to reconstruct the holding.

    Physical gold

    In Add position → Physical asset → Gold spot price, enter fine-gold weight in troy ounces, grams or kilograms. One troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams. The app retrieves gold spot in USD per troy ounce, converts the quantity and values it in the selected portfolio currency. Prices represent fine-gold spot value, without coin/bar premiums or dealing costs. Gold spot history is not yet supported. Manual pricing remains available; existing manual holdings only switch when you explicitly choose live spot pricing.

    Calendar layout

    Date pickers show Monday through Sunday by default. Choose Portfolio settings → Week starts on to switch between Monday and Sunday. This preference is saved with the portfolio and applies to its date pickers without changing recorded dates.

    Optional transaction history

    To find gaps across current positions, open Positions → History. It shows positions needing attention by default; clear that filter to see every position. The list separates absent or unapplied transactions, missing transaction details, and missing market inputs. Select a row to open its transaction review, holdings history or account journal. Checks use saved evidence and the recorded interval; matching balances alone cannot prove that no transactions are missing. Ordinary interior price gaps do not themselves make a position incomplete.

    Total quantity and average buy-in remain sufficient for current portfolio analysis. Open a position's Transactions view to start an empty list with Add buy or Add sell. Other transaction supports distributions, splits, transfers and fees. Save transaction retains incomplete work without changing the current holding. You can enter purchases and sales before deciding how much history you can reconstruct.

    Import PDFs accepts a batch of Consorsbank stock/ETF buy and sale statements for the selected position and account (up to 100 PDFs / 100 MB per batch, 20 MB per file). Files are read locally; original PDFs are not stored in history or sent to a service. Ready statements are hidden by default; Show ready statements reveals them. Problems and statements needing review come first, with newest dates first within each group. Ready, review and excluded counts always describe the whole batch; hiding a ready statement does not exclude it. Select a table row to inspect its amounts or change Include this transaction. Identical imports and invalid statements cannot be included. Similar trades start excluded; Import as a separate trade confirms only that statement, not the rest of the batch. Statement details include the identifier, taxes and settlement amount. If the position has no stored ISIN/WKN, check the document identifier yourself before saving; a filename or instrument name alone does not establish a match. When no identifier can be compared, I checked the instrument identifiers is required before saving. A known ISIN or WKN mismatch cannot be overridden. Legacy WKN-only statements are supported when the quoted price has an explicit currency; a currency-free quote still requires manual entry. For parsing failures, Statement details → Download masked layout report creates a local text report containing fixed financial labels, token types and reconciliation checks. It identifies which rows were parsed and which amounts match a settlement discrepancy without exporting those amounts; a match is a diagnostic clue, not an automatically applied correction. The report lists blocking parser issues and distinguishes exact settlement or quoted-price matches from nonzero differences within the rounding tolerance. Recognized order limits and tax calculation or allowance balances are labelled as informational and retain page/row references. They do not change execution prices or become transaction charges. Unexpected amounts on those rows still require review. It omits filenames, metadata and original names, identifiers, dates and amounts. Statement details also lists the page and visual row for each extracted field, including each fee/tax and conflicting candidates. Rows are counted from the top of each page. Compact source locations and the quoted price remain in saved transaction notes; raw extracted rows and original PDFs are not retained. Review the report before sharing it for troubleshooting; it does not upload anything or establish that the extracted financial values are correct. The Document column beside File in Statement details maps report numbers to local filenames for the current upload batch. Filenames stay out of the masked report. Reordering or changing the upload batch can change document numbers. Complete hexadecimal document-reference codes, including margin text, appear as <document-reference> in the report. They are not transaction amounts; other amounts on the same row still require classification.

    Identical files already imported anywhere in this portfolio are blocked. Similar trades are excluded by default and need explicit confirmation if they are distinct. Known instrument mismatches, ambiguous amounts, inconsistent totals, scanned or unsupported statements require manual entry. Corrections/reissues and currency conversion statements need separate review; this is not universal broker support. Unexplained transaction amounts, unsupported signs/refunds, repeated financial totals, extra amounts within a field, and a debit/credit direction contradicting the trade block saving. Confirming fee completeness cannot override these errors. Amounts without a currency also block when embedded in a financial field or accompanied by a charge label, including whole-number charges. Settlement rows recognize labelled German IBANs (compact or grouped) and legacy Konto-Nr. account numbers; other account layouts require manual entry. Separate tax-calculation bases are not tax charges; explicitly listed joint-account tax charges are each retained, even when their amounts agree. After settlement, unfamiliar single amounts inside a recognized tax-detail section and separately labelled ST quantities are supplementary context. Check statement shows their actual extracted text and page/row locations locally, beside the trade value, fees, taxes and settlement. They do not supply execution quantities, fees or taxes. This allows new explanatory labels without adding a parser rule for each one. Sections end at page boundaries, new trade headings or footer headings; arbitrary amounts elsewhere remain blocking. Explicit charges, corrections, malformed known fields and extra amounts remain subject to validation even inside supplementary sections.

    Reconciliation checks gross plus charges against a purchase debit, or gross minus charges against sale proceeds. It never fills a difference with an invented fee or tax. Matching totals cannot establish what an unfamiliar row means. Supplementary rows therefore prevent automatic charge confirmation: review the displayed rows and confirm that statement, or explicitly keep its charges unchecked.

    Listed fees/taxes are retained separately. A complete, unambiguous buy or sale with explicit debit/credit direction and an exactly reconciled settlement establishes the statement charges automatically. This includes fee-free savings plans, purchases with commissions, and sales with listed tax deductions. It requires fully parsed financial rows and no unresolved supplementary amounts; matching totals alone are insufficient. A buy is not assumed tax-free merely because it is a purchase. Separately billed costs need their own expense records.

    When a statement needs review, inspect the displayed additional rows. Tax bases, allowances and explanatory quantities are not additional payments. If all actual charges are included, choose Confirm statement charges. To retain uncertain history, choose Keep charges unchecked, or exclude the transaction. Decisions apply only to that statement; the save summary identifies any unchecked charges. Not listed means no amount was extracted, not that another tax is expected. An absent charge on an automatically reconciled or explicitly confirmed statement is shown as zero. No generic batch confirmation certifies other statements. Unsupported signs, refunds, fields and settlement discrepancies remain blocking where required by parser validation; confirmation never bypasses them.

    The original PDFs and additional-row text remain transient local review inputs. Saved history retains parsed transaction amounts and source locations, not raw rows or PDFs. Statement details contains document identifiers and the optional masked troubleshooting report. Statement amounts require recognized ISO currency codes; custody references and bank/page identifiers are not amounts. Unsupported historical currency codes require manual entry.

    Save transactions and review retains the batch atomically and opens the usual reconciliation below. It does not add shares to the current holding or activate history. Trade times order same-day imported records where available; otherwise upload order is retained and must be checked in the history review. Cancel/back before saving changes no portfolio data. Concurrent portfolio edits reject a stale save and preserve the uploaded draft for review.

    Edit transaction opens a form directly from this list. Choose the transaction, change its date, quantity, amount or fees, then Save changes. The original record is retained, and a correction affects the holding only after review and application. The list shows the latest version, with pending edits labelled. Money is displayed to two decimal places; the editor and calculations retain the full saved precision. Complex starting lots remain available in the advanced editor.

    When ready, choose Review against portfolio. By default, the selected records start with your first purchase and zero shares beforehand. This assumption is shown in the review; use Review options → Add starting balance if you already owned shares before the earliest recorded transaction. Applying history records the zero boundary at the end of the day before the earliest transaction or fee; merely opening the review saves nothing and does not invent a purchase. The internal zero boundary is not a transaction and is omitted from the transaction list and the Included transactions picker. Review options also lets you change which records are included and optionally link a cash account.

    Alternatively, Add starting balance records a dated, known quantity and optional total acquisition cost when earlier transactions are unavailable. It is optional, and saving it also leaves the current holding unchanged. A starting balance is an end-of-day boundary; its acquisition cost is separate from the market value used to start performance measurement. Earlier transactions are not inferred.

    The review compares shares, remaining acquisition cost and average buy-in with the current portfolio, and checks linked cash where applicable. Apply history accepts the displayed holding and linked cash changes as the authoritative ledger. No separate acceptance checkbox is needed. Backfilled shares are not added to the current holding a second time. Resolve discrepancies by correcting records or explicitly accepting the reconstructed balance; Keep portfolio unchanged leaves saved transactions available for later review. Replace balance starts a new interval for an already active history. Advanced record, lot and fee editing is available under Edit transactions, starting lots and fees; earlier records and corrections remain in its Retained evidence view.

    Double-click a transaction to edit it directly, or use its Edit button. The list opens newest first; click Date to reverse the order.

    To remove transactions, select rows in the Transactions table and choose Remove selected. The header checkbox selects all rows; starting balances are kept and edited separately. Confirm the selected entries before removal. Applied transactions also show the resulting holding and linked cash changes; invalid remaining histories cannot be applied. Unapplied entries leave portfolio balances unchanged. Original evidence is retained internally, and a removed PDF can be imported again. Re-uploading a PDF that is still present does not overwrite it. Removing the last transaction restores the empty entry view without an empty table.

    The quantity comparison uses the smaller of one billionth of a unit and one billionth of the recorded quantity. Comparable costs use a one-cent tolerance. These tolerances label differences; they never manufacture an adjustment. Unknown or incompatible converted costs are shown as unknown, not as agreement. Opening History automatically loads available dated prices and FX in the background, from the beginning of recorded history through today. A reconstructed purchase/sale history opens this view even before its first prices have loaded; snapshot-only positions keep the existing clean default. Saved observations are reused on later visits. Failed refreshes retain them and offer Retry prices. FX currencies come from saved transactions and observed price currency. Manual observations are preserved, and downloads never create transactions. Missing prices, FX or transaction records still need to be supplied. Personal value uses the shares held on each date and prices in that date's traded share units; split-normalized asset-chart prices cannot be reused blindly. Offline synthetic demos do not start automatic market requests.

    In Transactions → Review against portfolio, compare the reconstructed units and costs, then choose Apply history. For multiple holdings, use Accept this holding for each reviewed balance and Apply reviewed holdings to save the batch. Applying makes that history authoritative for the resulting position: a one-share history replaces a two-share balance with one share. Reapplying does not add those shares again.

    There is no separate history confirmation. New valuation dates and changes in chart period do not require reapproval. Gains and returns use the applied records; missing prices, FX, amounts or required starting balances still limit the affected metrics. A starting snapshot does not invent earlier transactions. Incomplete gain history identifies actual missing evidence or limited scope, with details in its tooltip. Supplied estimates remain labelled.

    Transaction entry offers Unit price, Trade value (excluding fees) and Total paid (including fees) for purchases or Total received (after fees) for sales. Always enter Fees separately: cash totals already include those fees, so they are not added or deducted again. Enter 0 for no fees; blank means unknown. There is no separate fee-confirmation checkbox for new transactions.

    When an earlier saved transaction has unconfirmed fees, Complete transaction fees in the review lets you supply or confirm them. It saves a correction and retains the original record without changing the holding until you apply history. Cost and average buy-in remain unknown when required costs or FX are missing; the review explains the missing input. Complete exchange rates accepts a manual rate for each missing date without using current FX. Applying history is disabled while the reconstructed cost or affected cash balance is unknown.

    Fees can have their own currency and date; Starting lots, fees and taxes supports multiple fees and recorded taxes. The old purchase-batch editor retains its established blank-fee-means-zero convention. Purchase fees enter acquisition cost once; sale fees reduce proceeds. Explicit taxes are separate expenses, not additions to the remaining FIFO acquisition cost.

    Sales use FIFO for analytical remaining costs. Enter actual opening lots where known. A snapshot's aggregate cost is not an invented set of purchase lots: a partial disposal from unknown opening lots leaves remaining cost unknown. Supply lot evidence or a cost row to reconcile the remaining total; that total does not establish individual lots for another partial disposal. A split row's quantity is the ratio of new units to old units. Internal transfers preserve known lot provenance and require a destination history for the same instrument. transfer_in and transfer_out record movements across the portfolio boundary; supply their dated market value separately from any known acquisition costs.

    A linked cash account is maintained once by the account journal. Trade and charge postings are derived from the selected securities records. Payments on or before an account's cash opening are already included in that opening. Explicit deferred claims remain separate until received or paid. Later settlement creates a claim until Accounts & futures → Cash & financing → Receive pending cash records receipt/payment. Do not also enter the same trade payment as a manual cash adjustment. Without linked cash, position-level purchases and sale proceeds cross the selected position's investment boundary.

    Activated holdings obtain current quantities and costs from history. Use Replace balance for a summary replacement, and Rebalance for category/target edits. Update balances continues to edit snapshot-only positions. Closed histories remain saved. External edits to an activated holding's original CSV balance are rejected rather than silently overriding its history.

    Legacy purchase batches remain unchanged. Copy batch to evidence makes a reviewable candidate with a stable reference to its original row. Cost recalculations may overlap purchases; inspect the selected rows and acknowledge any genuinely distinct matching transactions before activation.
